# A concise product walkthrough

## Positioning

REFLUENZ is a private digital atelier for creators and their core audiences. It brings publishing, a personal archive, direct conversation and membership into one considered space. The aesthetic supports the work instead of competing with it.

## 90-second demo

- **0–15 seconds — The promise.** Start on the landing page: “Own your aesthetic. Own your audience.” Explain that the product prioritizes creator relationships over advertising and algorithmic reach.
- **15–30 seconds — The member experience.** Enter the demo, read the featured entry, and bookmark it. Open the archive to show that it has been saved.
- **30–45 seconds — The relationship.** Visit Elena’s atelier and choose a demo membership. Explain that prices and transactions are illustrative. Open a circle-only entry.
- **45–60 seconds — The conversation.** Add a short question in Your circle. The demo stores it locally; real message delivery is a production integration.
- **60–80 seconds — The creator.** Switch to Creator, write or open a draft, preview it, and publish. Return to Member to see the entry in the feed.
- **80–90 seconds — The invitation.** Return to the introduction or play the film. End with “Your people. Your place.”

The film is a separate 30-second introduction. It can open a presentation before the live walkthrough.

## Be precise about evidence

The sample audience and monthly income in the studio are illustrative, not traction. The demo establishes the intended interaction and design quality. Real adoption, willingness to pay, retention and creator economics still need validation.

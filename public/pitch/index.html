03 — How it works

  1. 01

    Open your Studio

    A page of one's own, with a name, a banner, links and three tiers. It takes a minute.

  2. 02

    Publish the work

    Text, images or film, public or for members. Drafts stay private until they are ready.

  3. 03

    Grow your House

    People follow for free and join a door of the House to see everything. The creator keeps the relationship.

04 — Built to be trusted

Locked on the server,
not hidden in the page.

Member posts, photos and films never reach a reader without the right tier: the database enforces every rule. Data lives in the EU, and anyone can export or delete their account.
